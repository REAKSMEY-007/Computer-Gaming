Our story

From a two-person repair bench to a full custom-PC studio

We started out fixing friends' PCs and hunting down parts that actually lived up to the hype. Word spread, the bench got bigger, and we turned that obsession into Computer&Gaming — a store built around one simple idea: nobody should have to gamble on their next upgrade.

Today we assemble custom gaming PCs, source gear from 15+ trusted brands and back every purchase with warranty support. Each rig is stress-tested before it ships, and every part is chosen because we would put it in our own builds.

  • Every build hand-assembled and validated for part compatibility
  • Real technicians on support — no scripts, no runaround
  • Transparent pricing and honest advice, even when it's a cheaper part
